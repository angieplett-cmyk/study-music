# Study Music (no words, no ads)

A simple classroom music player with two playlists, **Calm Focus** and **Upbeat Study**.
Every track is instrumental. Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under
Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), plus
"Algorithms" by Chad Crouch (CC BY-NC 3.0). Full per-track credits are in `credits.html`.
Files were re-encoded to 96 kbps for streaming. No tracking, no ads, no student data.
